Building Taiky

I'm Ogundimbola Adesoji Raymond, a web developer, AI-assisted builder and entrepreneur. My work is about turning ideas into digital products people can actually use, not just writing code for its own sake.

Over the past year I've built my web development skills alongside modern AI-assisted, "vibe coding" workflows, using them to move from concept to working product faster than a traditional build cycle usually allows. I use that combination to turn ideas, concepts and business requirements into responsive, user-focused digital experiences.

I'm also the Founder & CEO of Taiky, a commerce platform I'm building to bring verified vendors, escrow-protected payments and live delivery tracking into one trusted ecosystem, launching first in Lagos.

Before development, I spent two years as a Front Desk Administrator, where I built communication, organisation and problem-solving skills through direct customer contact. I also spent a year in radio engineering at People's 93.1 FM in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, working in a technical broadcast environment. I hold a B.Sc. in Physics with Electronics from Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, which is where my analytical, systems-first approach to problem solving comes from.

Philosophy

“Technology should solve something.”

I don't start with the code. I start with a real problem, something that's slow, unclear or broken for someone. Code is the tool I use to fix it, not the point of the exercise. If a product doesn't make something easier, faster or more trustworthy for the person using it, it hasn't done its job yet, however well it's built.

Featured Project

Building Taiky.

Explore Taiky →

I didn't just want to build websites for other people. I wanted to build a product of my own, so I started Taiky.

The Problem

Commerce in Lagos is fragmented across separate apps and word-of-mouth trust. Buyers pay first and hope the seller is real, delivery day means guessing where an order is, and there's no single platform tying vendors, payments and delivery together with real protection.

The Vision

Taiky unifies verified vendors, escrow-protected payments and live-tracked delivery into one platform, so trust isn't something a buyer has to assume, it's built into every order.

My Role

As Founder & CEO, I own the product decisions, the user experience and the day-to-day work of turning the idea into something real, the same problem-first, build-it-yourself approach behind everything else in this portfolio.
